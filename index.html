Juno Kim

Associate Research Fellow · Korea Institute for Industrial Economics and Trade (KIET)
CVGitHub

I am an applied microeconomist working on migration, labor, and regional economics.

My research asks why people move and what their movement means for the places they leave and the places they join. I am particularly interested in how migration interacts with labor markets, public policy, and politics, and in how regions adjust when their population and economic base change.

I earned my Ph.D. in Economics from Georgetown University in 2024 and am currently an Associate Research Fellow at the Korea Institute for Industrial Economics and Trade (KIET), where I also conduct policy research on regional growth and balanced regional development.
